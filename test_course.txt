Advanced Machine Learning Basics

Chapter 1: Neural Networks
Neurons are the foundational building blocks of all neural networks. A neural network layers these neurons in sequences and passes weights forward to eventually classify inputs. The activation functions squash the output values into standard ranges.

Chapter 2: Backpropagation
Backpropagation relies on understanding how neurons work (Chapter 1) so it can calculate the gradients backwards. Gradients adjust the weights.

Chapter 3: Convolutional Networks
CNNs use spatial filters suited for images. They require an understanding of general Neural Networks and Backpropagation mechanics as a prerequisite to accurately interpret spatial gradient updates.
